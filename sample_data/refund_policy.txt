Company Refund Policy (effective 2024)

Standard customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase,
provided the product is unused. Refund requests must be submitted through
the support portal.

Enterprise customers on annual contracts signed before January 2023 are
covered under legacy terms: refunds are prorated based on unused months
remaining in the contract, capped at 6 months of fees. Contracts signed
January 2023 or later follow the standard 30-day policy regardless of
account tier.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
Enterprise customers with a dedicated account manager should route refund
requests through their AM rather than the support portal.
